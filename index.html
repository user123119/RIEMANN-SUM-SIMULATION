How does it work?

The function used in this simulation is:

f(x) = x²

We approximate the area under the curve using rectangles. When the number of rectangles increases, the rectangles become thinner and the approximation becomes closer to the exact integral.

∫₀¹ x² dx = 1/3 ≈ 0.3333

Notice what happens as the number of rectangles increases.

The rectangles become thinner and fit the curve more closely.

At the same time, the Riemann sum approaches the exact integral.
